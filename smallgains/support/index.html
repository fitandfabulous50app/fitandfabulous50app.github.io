Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Just open the app, tap Get started and pick your first small step.

How do I choose my small step?

Pick one tiny habit from the list, such as a 5-minute walk or a glass of water before lunch, or tap Add your own to write one that fits you. Start small. You can grow it later.

How do I mark my step as done?

On the Today screen, tap Done. A golden stone joins your path and counts as one more small win.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is lost. Your small wins are never reset. The app simply welcomes you back, and today is a fresh start. If a step feels too big, you can make it smaller.

What are keepsakes?

Keepsakes are rewards you earn at milestones, such as your first step or a number of small wins. Once earned, they stay on your keepsake shelf for good.

How do I change or turn off my daily reminder?

Change the reminder time in the app's settings (the gear at the top right). To turn reminders off, go to your iPhone's Settings > Notifications > Small Gains.

I am not getting my reminder.

Go to your iPhone's Settings > Notifications > Small Gains and make sure Allow Notifications is on. Also check that a Focus mode, such as Sleep or Do Not Disturb, is not silencing it.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your information stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Your progress is stored only on your phone, so deleting the app removes it.

Is this medical advice?

No. The app offers general wellness encouragement only. Please talk with your doctor before starting a new exercise, diet or health routine.
