Privacy Policy – Small Gains

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Small Gains ("the app") is offered by Raju Dhariwal, D.D.S. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains, in plain words, how the app handles your information.

We do not collect your data

The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. We do not use analytics or third-party tracking tools.

Everything stays on your iPhone

The small step you choose, the days you mark as done, your progress, your stepping-stone path, the keepsakes you earn and your reminder time are saved only on your phone. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes this information.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, that backup may include the app's information. Backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we cannot see them.

Your daily reminder

With your permission, the app shows one gentle reminder a day at a time you choose. The reminder is set up and delivered by your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server. Reminders are optional. You can change the time in the app, or turn reminders off at any time in Settings > Notifications > Small Gains.

Sharing a keepsake

If you tap Share on a keepsake, your iPhone's share sheet opens and you choose where it goes, such as Messages or Mail. The app shares only what you choose, only when you tap Share, and we do not see it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and the app does not collect personal information from anyone.

Not medical advice

The app offers general wellness encouragement only and is not medical advice. Please talk with your doctor before starting a new exercise, diet or health routine.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. If we ever add a feature that sends information off your phone, we will clearly describe it here and ask for your permission first.
